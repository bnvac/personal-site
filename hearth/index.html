Your machine, your keep.

Hearth is a Linux distribution for people who build things. It runs your AI on your own GPU, keeps your passwords and traffic to yourself, ships the tools for circuits and 3D printing, and describes the whole system in a handful of files you can carry to the next machine.

Everything runs here

A local voice assistant thinks on your graphics card. No cloud account, no subscription, nothing sent away to be answered.

Private by default

Tor for one app or all of them, and while it's on anything that would leak is refused, not let through. Secrets stay encrypted in git. Backups stay on your own drive.

Built for the bench

KiCad, FreeCAD, a slicer with real printer profiles, Klipper printers on the network, and a parts inventory that knows what's in your drawers.

It's built on NixOS, so the system is declared, not accumulated. Seventeen modules switch on and off with one command. Two machines, a desktop and a laptop, share one config and stay in step. Rebuild on new hardware and it's the same place.

The Armory

Each module is one feature, written once and switched on when you want it. Turning one off keeps it written, just unbuilt, so nothing is lost and anything comes back with a command.

The Workshop

What an evening on Hearth looks like. Each of these is a few keystrokes, because the pieces were built to know about each other.

I

Design a board, then see what you're missing

Lay it out in KiCad, export the BOM, and the parts inventory matches every line against your drawers. What's missing goes on a shopping list with the shop that delivered fastest last time.

II

Print the case, walk away

Slice with real Elegoo profiles. The printer is found on the network, and a desktop notification arrives when the print finishes or fails.

III

Ask the machine, out loud

Press a key and talk. Neb answers in a corner of the screen, running on the GPU through Vulkan. He can act on the desktop, and asks before typing or closing anything.

assistant · ai
IV

Pick up on the laptop

Files follow over Syncthing, your open tabs jump across with one command, bookmarks merge at login. No sync service in the middle.

V

Work where there's no internet

At login you join the local neighborhood web: pages, signed mail and files with whoever is on the same Wi-Fi. No server anywhere.

VI

Name a repo and a type (Rust, Go, Node, Python, Docker) and it's built and kept on its newest commit, but only one that builds.

The Codex

The handful of commands that run the place. Everything of yours lives under Super+Alt, so the defaults stay where you expect them.

  • mod on osintrebuild

    Switch a module on. mod off unbuilds it.

  • modtuitui

    Onboarding, modules, settings, AI model with a download bar.

  • rebuildnrs

    Update everything and switch, with a live progress bar.

  • nixmenuSuper+Alt+Space

    Every action on this system, by category.

  • neb-voiceSuper+Alt+N

    Talk to the assistant.

  • tormode onprivacy

    All traffic through Tor; anything that can't go is refused.

  • devlink tabssync

    Send your browser tabs to the other machine.

  • update-minedaily

    Move your own apps to their newest commit that builds.

  • debloat remove discordspace

    Drop an app from every module at once.

  • backup nowrestic

    Snapshot home and config to the external drive.

  • drivesSuper+Alt+M

    Mount and unlock drives. Nothing mounts by itself.

  • snow offSuper+Alt

    Stop the snow on the wallpaper. Not recommended.

The Gate

Start from any NixOS install. Keep its hardware file, take the rest from here.

# keep your hardware config, take Hearth's
sudo nixos-generate-config
sudo mv /etc/nixos /etc/nixos.orig
sudo git clone https://github.com/bnvac/nixosconfig /etc/nixos
sudo cp /etc/nixos.orig/hardware-configuration.nix /etc/nixos/
sudo nixos-rebuild switch
  1. Run modtui and choose Onboarding: desktop or laptop, a name, your modules, an AI model, and pairing with your other machine.
  2. Updates are nixup: a pull and a rebuild. Or let it happen weekly.
  3. To make it yours, fork the repository and clone the fork instead. nixup follows whichever you cloned.
