    I am a senior at Millis High School, outside Boston, class of 2027.

    Most of what I build starts the same way. I go looking for something, find that it does not exist or has quietly stopped being maintained, and write it myself. stdnt.xyz exists because the scholarship spreadsheets students pass around always die. backoffice exists because I kept buying the same ESP32 twice. artwork exists because I wanted a timer worth looking at for twenty-five minutes.

    The other half is research: synthetic-aperture radar on unmanned aircraft at MIT Beaver Works, a CubeSat team I led at Lincoln Laboratory, and a wireless brain–computer interface at Solderable on a Rev A grant.

    Away from a screen I captain a SeaPerch robotics team, run hackathons for Hack Club × NASA, and am working toward Eagle Scout. If you are building something, say hello.
