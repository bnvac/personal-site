    AlphaFold2 is less accurate on viral proteins

    Research project, 2026 · mentored by Dr. Blair G. Paul, Marine Biological Laboratory

    Across 233 solved structures, AlphaFold2’s median TM-score was 0.927 for viral proteins against 0.970 for human ones (p = 1.3 × 10−8). An alignment-free control with ESMFold more than doubled the gap. Code and data are public.
