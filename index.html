    Most of what I build starts the same way. I go looking for something, find that it does not exist or has quietly stopped being maintained, and write it myself. stdnt.xyz exists because the scholarship spreadsheets students pass around always die; backoffice exists because I kept buying the same ESP32 twice; artwork exists because I wanted a timer worth looking at for twenty-five minutes.

    The rest of my time goes to research and to teams. I captain a SeaPerch underwater robotics team, organise hackathons as a Hack Club × NASA Stardance ambassador, and serve on the alumni board of a youth grant programme that decides where its money goes. I am a senior at Millis High School, class of 2027.
