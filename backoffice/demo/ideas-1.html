Room climate monitor

Read temperature and humidity and put them somewhere you can see, on a board that can reach the network.

Suggested because you already have ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 and BME280 temp / humidity / pressure. You can print the case for it.

New · suggested your shelf · 3 days ago · an evening

Getting it in a box

Print it: a small wall box with vents over the sensor. Your bed is 225 × 225 × 265 mm. You have no calipers listed, so take dimensions from the datasheets and add a millimetre of slop.

Measure these yourself

Nothing on file records how big these are, and a case designed from a product photo never fits. You have no calipers listed, so take the dimensions from the datasheets and add a millimetre of clearance all round. Put the numbers in each part's specifications and they will be here next time.
